## 🎯 **Console Warnings Explained**

### **Harmless (Ignore These):**

**"LaTex" errors:**
- ❌ Xcode debugger noise
- ✅ Doesn't affect app
- ✅ Can be ignored

**"System gesture gate timed out":**
- ❌ iOS simulator warning
- ✅ Doesn't happen on real device
- ✅ Doesn't affect functionality

**"Application context data is nil":**
- ❌ WatchConnectivity initialization message
- ✅ Normal during setup
- ✅ Doesn't affect operation

**"XPC connection interrupted":**
- ❌ Simulator switching apps
- ✅ Normal behavior
- ✅ Reconnects automatically

### **Fixed:**

**"ATAudioSessionClientImpl.mm:281 activation failed":**
